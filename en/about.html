Fish is our trade.

We have sold fresh fish on Pucheni Street since 2020. Now we cook it too.

The fish shop

Atelierul Pescăresc, since 2020

The shop opened in 2020 at 157 Pucheni Street, in Sector 5. Since then it has brought in freshwater fish from Romania every day (carp, crucian carp, pike-perch, catfish, pike, sturgeon), sea fish from Greece and salmon from Norway, plus roe, smoked fish and seasonal seafood.

The restaurant

Jar și Plită

The idea is simple: the fish sold at the counter in the morning can be on the grill in the evening, a few steps away. No long journeys, no middlemen.

We cook two ways. Over embers (jar): whole fish or thick cuts, crisp skin, lemon and salt. On the griddle (plită): fillets, seafood and saramură, cooked fast over high heat.

How we cook

Three steps, no shortcuts

We choose

In the morning, at the counter. What is fresh goes on the day's menu; what is not, does not.

We clean

In the fish shop's kitchen, by people who have done this for years.

We put it on the fire

Over embers or on the griddle, with lemon, salt and olive oil. The fish does the rest.
