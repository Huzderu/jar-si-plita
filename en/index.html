The story

From the counter to the table

Atelierul Pescăresc opened in 2020 as a fish shop on Pucheni Street, in Sector 5, with a motto painted on the sign: "Fish from all over the world, for everyone". Customers came for Romanian carp, pike-perch, catfish and sturgeon, for Greek sea bream and mullet, for Norwegian salmon.

Jar și Plită is the next step: the same fish, cooked on the spot. A grill over embers, a griddle and a simple dining room where you eat fish the way it is eaten by the water.
